# ENDRIYA — Music Usage Policy

**Status:** PRE-LAUNCH POLICY  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-07

The presence of a music player in ENDRIYA does not grant a license to commercial songs or third-party recordings.

## Allowed production sources

- audio created and owned by the customer,
- audio for which the customer has explicit permission/license,
- platform-supplied audio only after ENDRIYA has recorded a commercial-use license or ownership basis,
- public-domain material where the recording and composition rights are both suitable for the intended use.

## Not automatically allowed

- songs copied from streaming platforms,
- YouTube/TikTok/Instagram audio simply because it is publicly playable,
- downloaded commercial recordings without permission,
- music marked “royalty-free” without checking the actual license and commercial/web redistribution terms.

## Operator workflow

Before adding customer-requested music, record at minimum:

1. track/title description,
2. source/provider,
3. customer confirmation or license basis,
4. whether the audio file is stored by ENDRIYA or linked externally,
5. any attribution or usage restrictions.

If rights are uncertain, publish the invitation without music until the issue is resolved. Music is an optional presentation feature and must never block access to wedding information.

## Technical requirements

- autoplay remains best-effort and browser-policy dependent,
- invitation content remains usable if audio fails,
- music assets must not contain secrets or personal guest tokens in URLs,
- future platform music libraries must be separately recorded in `ASSET_RIGHTS_REGISTER.md`.
